Community Guidelines

Welcome to Whisper Pages. We believe the writing matters, not the writer's identity. To keep this space safe, creative, and anonymous, you must abide by these simple rules:

  • Protect Anonymity: Do not post names, phone numbers, email addresses, or specific physical locations that could expose your own or someone else's identity.
  • Zero Harassment: Stalking, intimidation, targeted abuse, and harassment are strictly prohibited, particularly on the Shoutout Wall.
  • Age Gating: Honestly tag mature or 18+ content when publishing. Unrated publishing is blocked.
  • Harmful Content Gating: Any content related to exploitation, child harm, self-harm instructions, or extreme violence is filtered at submission time and permanently blocked.
  • Report Abuse: Use the "Report" flag on any content violating guidelines. Items reaching report thresholds are automatically hidden instantly.

Note: Accounts that remain inactive for 300 days are permanently and irreversibly destroyed, and their pen names are returned to the public pool.
